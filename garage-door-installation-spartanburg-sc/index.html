How Installation Day Works

1. Free on-site estimate

We measure the opening, headroom and side room, look at your opener and talk through styles, colors, windows and insulation. You get a written price.

2. Order & schedule

Your door is ordered from the manufacturer in your exact size and color, and we schedule installation as soon as it arrives.

3. Install in one day

Choosing the Right Door

Insulation: non-insulated single-layer steel is the budget option for detached garages; insulated doors (polystyrene or polyurethane, roughly R-6 to R-18) are quieter, stronger and cooler in a Carolina summer. Style: traditional raised panel, long panel, carriage-house with decorative hardware, or modern flush and full-view glass. Windows: a row of windows brightens the garage and dresses up the front of the house. Color: white and almond are classic; black, charcoal and woodgrain finishes are the most requested upgrades right now. Browse door styles and prices or photos of our installs.

New Door FAQ

How much does a new garage door cost in Spartanburg?

Single 8×7 insulated steel doors start at $1,950 installed, including tear-out, new tracks and springs, balancing and haul-away. Double doors are priced by size, insulation, windows and style.

How long does garage door installation take?

Most single and double doors are installed in one day — usually 3 to 5 hours on site.

Do you haul away the old door?

Yes. Tear-out and haul-away of the old door, track and springs are included in our installed prices.

Should I get an insulated garage door in South Carolina?

If the garage is attached, has a room above it, or you use it as a workshop or gym, yes. Insulated doors are quieter, stiffer and noticeably cooler in summer.

Can I keep my existing opener?

Often, if it is in good shape and has working safety sensors. We will check it and tell you honestly.
