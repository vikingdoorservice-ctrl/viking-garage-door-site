Common Boiling Springs Garage Door Problems

Broken torsion springs

The loud bang, then a door that won't lift. The most common call we get in 29316 by a wide margin. Replaced same day with 25,000-cycle springs — more than double the builder's.

Spring replacement →

Door off the track

Snapped cable, a bump from a vehicle, or a drum that slipped. The door hangs crooked and is genuinely dangerous. Leave it and call us.

Opener won't respond

Stripped nylon gear, dead logic board, or a remote that lost its code. We repair LiftMaster, Chamberlain, Genie, Craftsman and Linear units, and replace what isn't worth repairing.

Opener installation →

Door reverses before closing

Photo-eye sensors knocked out of alignment — a bike, a trash can, a spider web. Fifteen-minute fix in most cases.

Loud, rough, shaking door

Worn steel rollers and dry hinges. Left alone, a rough door destroys cables and springs years early. Nylon roller swap makes it quiet immediately.

Tune-up →

Cold, water and leaves coming in

Cracked bottom seal or settled concrete. Cheap to fix, and it stops the mice.
