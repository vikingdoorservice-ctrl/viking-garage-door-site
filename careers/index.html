The Job

Garage Door Installer / Service Technician

Viking Garage Door is a growing, owner-operated garage door company based in Boiling Springs. We install and service residential and light commercial doors across Spartanburg, Greenville and the rest of the Upstate. We are looking for one more good hand.

What you will do

  • Install residential and light commercial garage doors
  • Replace torsion and extension springs, cables, rollers and hinges
  • Put doors back on track and repair bent track
  • Install and program LiftMaster and Chamberlain openers
  • Run a clean, safe job site and talk straight with customers

What we are looking for

  • Mechanically minded and comfortable with hand and power tools
  • Valid driver license and a clean record
  • Able to lift 75 lbs and work on a ladder
  • Shows up on time, every time
  • Garage door experience is a plus — we will train the right person

Why work here

  • Local routes. Spartanburg County and the Upstate — you sleep in your own bed every night.
  • Steady work. Repairs do not stop for the economy; a broken door has to be fixed.
  • Real training. Torsion systems, low-headroom and high-lift conversions, commercial roll-ups.
  • You talk to the owner. Small crew, decisions made same day.
