Questions About These Doors

Do you ship to my state?

Yes. We ship the door and SOMMER opener kit to any state. Delivery is $1.60 per mile from our warehouse in Spartanburg, SC. Text us your ZIP code and we'll confirm the delivery cost and date.

Who installs the door outside South Carolina?

Any experienced garage door installer or contractor can install it. Each door comes with the manufacturer's installation instructions, and you can call us with questions during the install.

Is the Upstate installed price really all-in?

Yes. The price covers the door, the SOMMER opener with keypad, all tracks and hardware, and professional installation with setup and adjustment. The only extras are sales tax and anything unusual we find on site — like removing an old door, reframing the opening, or adding an outlet — and we tell you about those before any work starts.

How fast can you install?

These doors are in stock locally, so there is no factory lead time. After a free measurement we can usually schedule installation quickly — call or text for the next open date.

Will it fit my garage?

These are standard 9×8, 16×8 and 18×8 openings with standard-lift track. We measure your opening, headroom and side room for free before you order, so there are no surprises.

Can I keep my current opener?

Yes. Ask us for a door-only installed price. Most customers take the package because the SOMMER opener comes matched to the door and ready to go.

Do you have other sizes or colors?

The prices on this page are for the in-stock matte black doors. Other sizes, colors and styles can be ordered — send us your opening size and we will quote it.
