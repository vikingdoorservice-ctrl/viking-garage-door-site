Replace One Section or the Whole Door?

Most residential garage doors are built from four or five horizontal sections. If one of them is dented, cracked or rotted, it can often be replaced on its own — as long as the manufacturer still makes that model, size and color. We identify your door (brand, model, panel design and color), check availability with the supplier and give you an honest comparison: the cost of a new section against the cost of a new insulated door. On older or discontinued doors, or when more than one or two sections are damaged, a new door is usually the better buy.

When a Panel Needs Attention

  • A section is dented or bowed from a car, mower or ball
  • The bottom section is rusted through or rotted
  • A panel is cracked along the hinge line or stiles
  • The door no longer seals at the bottom or lets light in
  • Storm or fallen-limb damage

A badly bent panel can bind in the tracks and strain the opener and cables. If the door does not move smoothly, leave it closed and call us.

How Panel Replacement Works

Identify the door

We photograph the label and panel profile to match brand, model, size and color — Wayne Dalton, Clopay, Amarr, Garaga and others.

Order the right section

Matching sections are ordered from the manufacturer. Until they arrive, we make sure your door is safe to use.

Install & rebalance

We swap the section, replace hinges and rollers as needed, and rebalance the door.

What It Costs

Panel prices depend entirely on the door brand, model and size, so we quote after identifying your door — the assessment is free. If a section is not available, new insulated doors start at $1,950 installed with the old door hauled away.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I replace just one garage door panel?

Often, yes — if the manufacturer still makes your door model in the same size and color. We check before quoting.

Will a new panel match the old ones?

It will match in design and factory color. On older doors, the new section can look slightly brighter until it weathers.

Is it cheaper to replace one panel or the whole door?

For one section on a newer door, a panel is usually cheaper. For two or more sections, or older doors, a new door often costs only a little more and comes with a new warranty.

Does insurance cover a damaged garage door?

Homeowner policies often cover vehicle or storm damage. We can provide a written estimate and photos for your claim.
