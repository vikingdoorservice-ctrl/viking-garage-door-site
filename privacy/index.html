Privacy Policy

Viking Garage Door LLC · Last updated: August 19, 2026

This policy explains what information Viking Garage Door LLC (“Viking Garage Door,” “we,” “us”) collects when you contact us or use vikingdoor.net, how we use it, and the choices you have. We are a garage door repair and installation company serving Upstate South Carolina.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you submit a form on our website, respond to one of our ads on Facebook or Instagram, call us, or text us, we collect the details you provide — typically your name, phone number, email address, service address, and a description of the work you need.
  • Information collected automatically. When you visit our website we receive standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type, pages viewed, and the site or ad that referred you.
  • We do not collect payment card details through this website. Payment is handled in person or through a separate invoice.

How we use it

  • To respond to your request, schedule service, and provide an estimate.
  • To contact you about your job — by phone, text message, or email.
  • To keep records of work performed, warranties, and invoices.
  • To measure which ads and pages bring us service requests, so we do not waste money on advertising that does not work.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Text messages

If you give us your mobile number, we may text you about your service request, appointment time, or estimate. Message and data rates may apply. You can stop these messages at any time by replying STOP or telling us directly. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any service.

Cookies and advertising tools

Our website uses tools that place cookies or similar identifiers in your browser:

  • Meta Pixel (Facebook and Instagram) — tells us when a visit or a service request came from one of our ads, and lets us show our ads to people who visited our site.
  • Google tag — measures which Google ads and searches lead to service requests.

These providers process the data under their own privacy policies. You can limit ad tracking through your browser settings, your device settings, or through Facebook ad preferences and Google ad settings.

Who we share information with

We share information only where it is needed to run the business:

  • Technicians and subcontractors performing your job.
  • Service providers that host our website, send our messages, process invoices, or manage our scheduling and customer records.
  • Advertising and analytics platforms, as described above.
  • When required by law, or to protect our rights, safety, or property.

How long we keep it

We keep service records, invoices, and warranty information for as long as needed to support the work we performed and to meet tax and legal obligations. Inquiries that do not lead to a job are kept only as long as they are useful for follow-up.

Your choices

You may ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. To do so, contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable time. If you no longer want to hear from us, tell us and we will stop contacting you.

Children

Our services are directed to property owners and managers. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
