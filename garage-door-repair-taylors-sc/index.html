What We See on Taylors Garage Doors

A lot of Taylors homes were built between the 1960s and the 1990s, and many still have the original door — often an uninsulated steel or wood door hung on extension springs that stretch along the ceiling tracks. Those springs wear out without much warning and, without safety cables, can whip loose when they break. When we fix one, we will tell you honestly whether it makes sense to convert to a modern torsion spring system. Newer homes toward Paris Mountain tend to have builder-grade doors and chain-drive openers that are now reaching the end of their life.
