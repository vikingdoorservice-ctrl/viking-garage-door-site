Why an Annual Garage Door Service Pays for Itself

Springs Last Longer

Dry, unbalanced doors wear out springs years early. Lubrication and a proper balance check protect the most expensive wear parts on your door.

Catch Problems Early

Frayed cables, worn rollers and loose hardware are cheap to fix when caught at a tune-up — and expensive when they fail on a Sunday night.

A Quieter, Smoother Door

Most squeaking, grinding and rattling disappears after one professional service visit.

Safety You Can Verify

We test the auto-reverse and photo sensors — the systems that protect kids, pets and car hoods — and calibrate opener force to spec.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a garage door tune-up cost?

A flat $120 in the Spartanburg and Greenville area — lubrication, balance test, hardware tightening, and a complete spring, cable and opener safety check.

What is included in a garage door tune-up?

Lubrication of springs, hinges, rollers and tracks; door balance test; hardware tightening; safety-sensor and auto-reverse test; opener force calibration; and a wear inspection of springs, cables and pulleys with a written condition report.

How often should a garage door be serviced?

Once a year for a typical home. Doors that cycle many times a day — busy families or rental properties — benefit from a tune-up every six months.

Do you service all garage door brands?

Yes. We maintain and repair all major residential garage door and opener brands across Spartanburg and Greenville counties.
