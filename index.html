My spring just broke. Can I still open the door?

No. The springs carry the entire 150–250 lb weight of the door. With one broken, the opener is holding all of it on a plastic gear. Do not force it, do not pull the emergency release with the door up, park outside and call us.

How long should a garage door spring last?

Builder-grade springs are rated for about 10,000 cycles — roughly seven years at six to eight openings a day. The high-cycle springs we install are rated around 25,000 cycles, about double the life for a small difference in price.
