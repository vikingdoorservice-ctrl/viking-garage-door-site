Common Amarr Problems We Repair

Amarr is one of the most common garage door brands in new construction across Upstate South Carolina. If your home in Boiling Springs, Duncan, or Greer was built in the last 15 years, there's a good chance you have an Amarr door — often a builder-grade model installed as quickly as possible.

Builder-grade Amarr doors work fine for years, but the original installation is where problems usually start. Most of these are straightforward repairs. We balance the door correctly after any spring work — the step that gets skipped most often, and the reason so many "repaired" doors break the opener a year later.

What we see most

  • Broken torsion springs (typically 8–12 years in)
  • Springs that were undersized for the door from day one
  • Worn nylon or steel rollers causing noise and shaking
  • Loose or stripped hinges
  • Cables frayed at the bottom bracket
  • Struts missing on wider doors, causing the top panel to bow
  • Panel damage from a car bumper

Amarr Door Replacement and Upgrades

If you're replacing a builder door, this is the moment to upgrade. Amarr's Lincoln, Oak Summit, Classica, and Hillcrest lines offer insulated construction, better wind ratings, and real curb appeal. An insulated door makes a noticeable difference in a garage that sits under a bedroom or next to a living space during a South Carolina summer.

We also install Amarr doors on new construction and additions. Builders and general contractors in Spartanburg County: we offer clean, on-schedule installs and can handle multiple doors per site.

Amarr Questions

My Amarr door is only 10 years old — why did the spring break?

Spring life is measured in cycles, not years. A standard builder spring is rated around 10,000 cycles; two cars in and out daily uses that up in roughly 7 years. We can install high-cycle springs that last several times longer.

Can you replace just one damaged Amarr panel?

Often yes, if the model is still in production and the rest of the door is in good condition. We'll check availability and give you both options — panel replacement versus a new door.

Do you install Amarr doors from a different supplier?

Yes — we install the door, not the paperwork. If you already have a door on order, call us for the install.
