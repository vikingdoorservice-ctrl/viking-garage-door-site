Which Amarr Door Should You Choose?

  • Detached garage or tight budget — Lincoln LI1000 or Heritage HR1000.
  • Attached garage in the Carolina heat — insulated Lincoln or Heritage 3000 (R-9.05). The most popular choice.
  • Room above the garage, home gym or workshop — Olympus with polyurethane insulation, R-19.40.
  • Modern farmhouse or new build — EOS smooth flush in black or white, or Northwoods wood-tone.
  • Carriage-house curb appeal — Classica or Carriage Court.

Amarr Garage Door FAQ

What does the installed price include?

The Amarr door in white with standard tracks, torsion springs, hinges and rollers, plus installation by our crew. Colors, windows and upgrades are quoted before you order.

Which Amarr door is best for a hot South Carolina garage?

If the garage is attached or has a room above it, go insulated. Lincoln LI2000 is the budget step up, Lincoln or Heritage 3000 (R-9.05) is the most popular, and Olympus (R-19.40) is the warmest steel door in the lineup.

Do you install Amarr doors in Hendersonville and Asheville, NC?

Yes. We are based in Boiling Springs, SC and install Amarr doors across the Upstate and in Hendersonville and Asheville.

My Amarr door is only 10 years old — why did the spring break?

Spring life is measured in cycles, not years. A standard builder spring is rated around 10,000 cycles; two cars in and out daily uses that up in roughly 7 years. We can install high-cycle springs that last several times longer.
