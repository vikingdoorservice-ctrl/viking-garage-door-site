Why Homeowners Choose Garaga

Garaga is a premium Canadian manufacturer known for heavily insulated, tightly built doors. If you want a garage that stays comfortable year-round, a door that closes quietly, or a design that actually matches your home, Garaga is worth a serious look.

For homes in Spartanburg County with a workshop, home gym, or bedroom above the garage, an insulated Garaga door is one of the few upgrades you feel immediately.

What sets Garaga apart

  • Polyurethane-injected panels with high R-values — real insulation, not a foam sheet glued on
  • Thermal breaks and weather seals that keep humidity and heat out
  • Wide range of designs: Standard+, Cambridge, Eastman, Princeton, and modern styles
  • Custom sizes for odd openings and tall garages
  • Strong panels that work well with high-lift and side-mount opener setups

Installation Done Right

A premium door only performs if it's installed precisely. We measure the opening, headroom, side room, and backroom before ordering, set the tracks to the door's specs, size the springs to the actual door weight, and adjust the seal so it closes tight along the whole bottom. Every Garaga install is done by the owner.

If your garage has a high ceiling, ask about a high-lift conversion with a wall-mounted LiftMaster opener — Garaga doors handle it well, and you get your ceiling space back for storage or a car lift.

Garaga Questions

Is a Garaga door worth the price over a builder-grade door?

If the garage is attached, used daily, or has living space above it — usually yes. The insulation, quieter operation, and longer service life add up. For a detached storage garage, a standard insulated door may be enough. We'll tell you either way.

How long does a Garaga door take to arrive?

Lead times vary by model and color. We'll give you a realistic date when we quote.

Can Garaga doors be installed high-lift?

Yes. Garaga panels are stiff enough for high-lift tracks, and we pair them with a LiftMaster wall-mount opener.
