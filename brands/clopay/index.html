Clopay Repairs We Handle

Clopay is the largest garage door manufacturer in the U.S., and it shows in Upstate South Carolina — from basic non-insulated doors on older homes in Spartanburg to Gallery and Coachman carriage-style doors in newer neighborhoods around Boiling Springs and Greer. Whatever Clopay model you have, we repair it and, if it's time, replace it.

Older Clopay doors with extension springs (the long springs along the horizontal tracks) are a safety concern if the safety cables were never installed. We check that on every visit and recommend a torsion conversion where it makes sense.

What we fix most

  • Broken torsion or extension springs
  • Cables off the drum, frayed, or snapped
  • Rollers worn or popped out of track
  • Door off track after a bump or a broken cable
  • Bent or cracked panels and struts
  • Weather seal and bottom rubber replacement
  • Noisy operation — often rollers, hinges, or a bent track

Clopay Door Replacement

Clopay offers a wide range: Classic Steel for value, Gallery and Coachman for carriage-house looks, Modern Steel for contemporary homes, and insulated Intellicore models for comfort and quiet. We help you pick the right one based on the garage's use, your home's style, and your budget — then install it correctly, with springs sized to the actual door weight and tracks set to Clopay's spec.

Builders and property managers: we can quote and install multiple Clopay doors per site with a consistent look across units.

Clopay Questions

How do I know which Clopay model I have?

Look for a sticker on the inside of a panel or the end stile — it usually lists the model and serial number. Send us a photo and we'll identify it.

Can a dented Clopay panel be replaced?

Often yes, if the model is current. On discontinued designs, a full door may be the only clean option.

Do you install Clopay doors bought from Home Depot or Lowe's?

Yes. Have the door delivered and call us for installation.

Is Intellicore insulation worth it in South Carolina?

For attached garages, yes — it cuts heat transfer and makes the door noticeably quieter.
