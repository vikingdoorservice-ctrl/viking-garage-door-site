LiftMaster Openers We Install

LiftMaster is the professional-grade opener line we install more than any other. Quiet belt drives, battery backup, built-in Wi-Fi and myQ, and the 8500W wall-mount unit that frees up your entire ceiling — LiftMaster covers every setup we see in Spartanburg County homes.

Every install includes safety sensor alignment, force and travel setup, and myQ app connection so you can check and close the door from your phone.

Models

  • Belt drives (87504 / 85503) — quiet, Wi-Fi, battery backup, built-in camera on select models. The go-to for a garage under a bedroom.
  • Wall-mount / jackshaft (8500W / 98022) — mounts beside the door instead of on the ceiling. Required for high-lift doors and ideal for tall garages, car lifts, or overhead storage.
  • Chain drives — durable and budget-friendly for detached garages and workshops.
  • Commercial operators — for small shops, warehouses, and roll-up doors.

High-Lift and Side-Mount Projects

This is our specialty. If your garage has 10–14 ft ceilings, we can convert the door to high-lift tracks so it rises nearly vertical and parks above the opening, then install a LiftMaster 8500W on the wall. You get the full ceiling back. We handle the track modification, spring recalculation, and opener install as one project.

LiftMaster Repair

We'll tell you when a repair makes sense and when a 15-year-old unit is better replaced.

What we repair

  • Opener runs but door doesn't move (usually a broken spring, not the opener)
  • Motor hums but won't lift — stripped gear
  • Door reverses immediately — sensor or force setting
  • Remote and keypad programming, myQ reconnection
  • Logic board, travel module, and trolley replacement on repairable units

LiftMaster Questions

Belt drive or chain drive?

Belt if the garage is attached or under living space. Chain for detached garages where noise doesn't matter.

Can I use a wall-mount opener on my current door?

It needs a torsion spring system, a solid front-mount setup, and enough side room. We check this on-site — most doors qualify or can be adapted.

Does the 8500W work with high-lift?

Yes — it's the standard opener for high-lift doors.

My LiftMaster opener stopped working. Is it the opener or the spring?

Pull the red release cord and lift the door by hand. If it's very heavy, it's the spring. If it lifts easily, it's the opener.
