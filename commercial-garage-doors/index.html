Doors for Shops, Bays and Metal Buildings

Besides homes, we install and service sectional garage doors for small businesses across the Upstate — auto and repair shops, contractor and farm shops, metal buildings, storage and multi-bay facilities, and businesses that want full-view aluminum and glass doors for a modern storefront look. When a commercial door is down, your business is down, so we schedule repairs fast and keep the common springs, cables and rollers on the truck.

What We Install and Service

  • Insulated and non-insulated steel sectional doors
  • Full-view aluminum and glass doors for showrooms and shops
  • Doors for metal buildings and pole barns, sized to the opening
  • Heavy-duty and wall-mount (jackshaft) openers
  • Spring, cable, roller and track repair on commercial sectional doors

Have several doors? Ask about a scheduled maintenance visit — lubrication, balance and safety checks on every bay at once.

How a Commercial Project Works

Free site visit

We measure each opening, check headroom and side room, and talk through insulation, windows and opener options.

Clear written quote

One price per door, including track, springs, hardware, opener and haul-away of old doors.

Scheduled install

We plan the work around your hours to keep bays open as much as possible.

What It Costs

Commercial doors vary widely by size, insulation, glass and opener, so every project is quoted on site for free. Repairs on commercial sectional doors follow the same upfront-pricing rule as our residential work: the price before the work.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work on roll-up sheet doors?

We specialize in sectional doors. Tell us what you have and we will let you know honestly whether we are the right fit.

Can you install doors on a new metal building?

Yes. We install sectional doors in framed openings on metal buildings and pole barns, sized to your opening and headroom.

Do you install full-view glass doors?

Yes — aluminum full-view doors with clear, frosted or tinted glass for shops, showrooms and restaurants.

Do you offer maintenance for multiple doors?

Yes. We can service all your doors in one visit on a schedule that suits your business.
